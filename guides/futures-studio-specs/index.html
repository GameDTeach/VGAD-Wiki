Futures Studio Hardware Specs

What the LED wall is made of, how big it is, and what drives it.

Reach for this when you need a number — a resolution to type into a config, or the panel pitch for working out how close the camera can get before you see the pixels.

The main LED wall

  • Width10.2 m measured straight across the room
  • Height3.5 m
  • Columns21, each turned 2.5° from its neighbour
  • Panels per column7
  • Total panels147
  • Resolution4200 × 1400 pixels
Why the width looks like two different numbers

Twenty-one panels at 500 mm each is 10.5 m of panel. Because the wall is curved, the straight-line distance from one end to the other is shorter than the panel run — that works out at 10.17 m, which is the 10.2 m in the original spec.

Both numbers are right, they just measure different things. Use 10.5 m when you're counting panels, and 10.2 m when you're working out whether something fits in the room.

Hardware

  • LED panelsAbsen PL2.5 Pro, 2.5 mm pixel pitch
  • Panel size500 × 500 mm, 200 × 200 pixels each
  • Video processorsBrompton Tessera SX40 × 2
  • Render nodes2, one per side of the wall — see Setting up nDisplay
What pixel pitch means for your shot

2.5 mm is the distance between one LED and the next. Get the camera close enough and you start seeing individual pixels, or moiré patterning where the LED grid beats against the sensor grid.

There's no single safe distance — it depends on lens, sensor and aperture. If the wall starts to shimmer in the viewfinder, backing the camera off or opening up the aperture to throw the wall slightly out of focus usually clears it.
